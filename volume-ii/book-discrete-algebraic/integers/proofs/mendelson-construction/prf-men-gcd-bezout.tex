\begin{theorem*}[Mendelson 5.3 --- GCD and Bezout form]
For integers \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\), not both zero, a greatest common divisor
exists. Moreover, the greatest common divisor may be written in Bezout form
\[
  d = x\alpha+y\beta
\]
for some integers \(x,y\).
\end{theorem*}
