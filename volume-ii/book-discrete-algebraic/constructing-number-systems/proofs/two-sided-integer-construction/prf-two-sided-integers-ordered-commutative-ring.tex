\begin{theorem*}[The two-sided construction gives an ordered commutative ring]
With addition, negation, multiplication, and order defined by the two-sided
successor construction, the resulting system satisfies the laws of an ordered
commutative ring.
\end{theorem*}

\begin{proof}[Professional Standard Proof]
\LRAProofBodyStart
Define addition by recursion on the second input: adding zero is the identity,
adding a positive point iterates successor, and adding a negative point
iterates predecessor. The inverse laws prove the boundary equations
\[
x+\mathsf{succ}(y)=\mathsf{succ}(x+y),
\qquad
x+\mathsf{pred}(y)=\mathsf{pred}(x+y).
\]
Two-sided induction then proves the left identity, successor transport,
predecessor transport, commutativity, and associativity of addition.

Define negation by exchanging the positive and negative rays and fixing zero.
This gives \(-(-x)=x\) and \(x+(-x)=0\). Define multiplication by repeated
addition along the right input, using repeated addition of \(-x\) on the
negative ray. The additive lemmas prove the successor and predecessor
multiplication equations, and two-sided induction proves commutativity,
associativity, and distributivity.

Finally, define \(x<y\) when \(y+(-x)\) lies on the positive ray. The
disjointness of the three parts gives trichotomy and irreflexivity; closure of
the positive ray under addition and multiplication gives transitivity and the
order compatibility laws.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Detailed Learning Proof]
\LRAProofBodyStart
The proof is long because every familiar algebra law must be recovered from
the ray motion. Addition is not symmetric by definition, so commutativity is a
theorem. Multiplication is not symmetric by definition, so commutativity is
again a theorem. Order is not imported from the usual integers; it is defined
by positivity of a difference, so its totality and compatibility with
arithmetic must also be proved. These are exactly the construction-verification
obligations from the ground rules of the chapter.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark*}[Proof structure]
Build addition first, prove its transport lemmas, build negation, build
multiplication from repeated addition, then define order from the positive ray.
\end{remark*}
